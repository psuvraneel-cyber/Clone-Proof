# Code of Conduct

CloneProof adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone taking part in this project's
issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep
criticism focused on code and ideas rather than people. Harassment,
discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publishing others' private
information are not accepted.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer
privately through the contact options on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/psuvraneel-cyber). Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation
in line with the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
